# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals and families managing personal finances, tracking budgets, and seeking AI-driven insights to improve their financial health.

## Product Purpose

A SaaS application that connects family members to a shared financial dashboard, providing a clear summary of balances, categorized expenses, goals, and AI assistant insights.

## Brand Personality

Minimalist, clean, professional, and highly utilitarian.

## Anti-references

Generic "AI/SaaS" aesthetics (e.g., purple gradients, endless glassmorphism).

## Design Principles

- Clarity over decoration: Data and insights should be front and center without unnecessary visual noise.
- Utility first: The interface must feel like a precision tool for financial management.
- Crisp execution: Rely on strong typography, ample negative space, and subtle, purposeful shadows to create depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices: high contrast for readability and clear focus states for navigation.
